Learn From Landmark Cases

Understand how Indian courts have ruled in situations similar to yours.

Domestic Violence
Indra Sarma v. V.K.V. Sarma (2013)
Supreme Court of India · 2013
Landmark ruling extending PWDVA protection to live-in relationships, establishing that cohabitation need not be marital for domestic violence protections to apply.
PWDVA Section 2(f)
Cyber Crime
Shreya Singhal v. Union of India (2015)
Supreme Court of India · 2015
Struck down Section 66A of the IT Act as unconstitutional, protecting freedom of speech online and setting limits on arbitrary arrest for online content.
IT Act Article 19
Assault
State of Maharashtra v. Madhkar Narayan (1991)
Supreme Court of India · 1991
Established that every woman has the right to privacy and her testimony of assault is credible on its own — no corroboration required for conviction.
IPC 354 Women's Rights
